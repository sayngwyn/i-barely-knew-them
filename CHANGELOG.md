# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release. Pick how well someone must know you before their death sends you an inheritance letter and gold: Friend (the base game), Confidant, Ally, Lover, or Off (no letters at all).
- One small plugin per choice; the installer installs the one you pick. No scripts, no SKSE.
- Works with or without the Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch (it carries that patch's one change to this quest). Load it after the patch.
- Tested on Skyrim 1.6.1170 and 1.7.104.
